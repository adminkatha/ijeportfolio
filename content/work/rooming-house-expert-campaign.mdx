## Creative approach

The winning ad is an illustrated, poster-style creative: a cartoon character in a suit beside a house, the headline "8 Rooms. $2,200/week!" and the promise "Convert Your Property Into a Rooming House! Download The Guide Here!". The primary text makes one claim and asks one question: "Turn 1 Property into 2–3x Rental Income – See If Yours Qualifies!". Under the image: "Convert. Rent. Three talking-head video ads promote the same guide from different angles: why a big weekly rent figure isn't the number that matters, why more rent doesn't automatically mean a better investment, and what is actually left after costs. Each ends on a mock-up of the guide.

## Setup

- **Objective:** leads, with a Meta instant form.
- **Follow-up:** the form's additional action books a free strategy call.
- **Placements:** feeds and in-stream reels; stories, status, reels, search results, apps and sites; Facebook search results.
- **Attribution:** 7-day click or 1-day view.

## Results

From the campaign's one-page report, 1 August to 1 September 2026 (Australian dollars; attribution: 7-day click or 1-day view):

- **257 leads** at **A$25.01** per lead, from **A$6,427.45** of spend (a daily budget of A$200).
- **78,091 people** reached and **223,501** impressions, a frequency of **2.86**.
- **1,837 link clicks**: a link click-through rate of **0.82%** at **A$3.50** per click. CPM: **A$28.76**.
- **14%** of link clicks became leads, about 7 clicks per lead.

The report's own reading: lead volume strong, cost per lead good at that volume, frequency healthy with no obvious fatigue yet. The one to watch was the 0.82% link click-through rate, which leaves room to improve the creative's hook.
