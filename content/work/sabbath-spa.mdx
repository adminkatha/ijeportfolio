## Architecture

The portal is a Next.js app (React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS). Guests get booking, waivers, memberships and in-room ordering; staff sign in to a management back office.

- **Data and sign-in:** Supabase (its database, with authentication through Supabase's server-side helpers for Next.js).
- **Forms:** React Hook Form with Zod validation.
- **Email:** Resend and Nodemailer.

## Implementation

**The website:** an intro over a collage of the spa's rooms ("Embrace the Gift of Rest"), a welcome, the signature massages and the Sabasu café, with **Book Now** in the header.

**For guests**

- **Book a Session:** reserve an appointment and choose the service and the therapist.
- **Digital Waiver:** a health intake form and a liability waiver.
- **Membership:** discounts, VIP packages and priority booking.
- **Sabasu:** order coffee, pasta and pastries to the room.

**For staff**

- **Bookings ledger:** every booking per branch, filtered by brand (Sabbath or Le Nails), searchable by customer, date or service, with CSV export.
- **Clients, Staff, Payments and Waivers**, plus **Audit Logs**.
